How it works

1. Pick a subdomain and a plan — Store, Marketplace, or both.
2. You become the store's Admin — add your own categories and products.
3. Invite Employees (or additional Admins) to help fulfil orders and bookings.
4. Clients register on your subdomain and shop only your catalog.

Every store's data — users, catalog, orders — is fully isolated from every other store.

Join Hadam Marketplace

List your existing business in Hadam's Marketplace for visibility — no Hadam store, subdomain, or account needed. Purchases are handled entirely on your own website.

How it works

1. Submit your business information — no Hadam store or subdomain is created.
2. Your business appears in Hadam's Marketplace directory with your logo, description, and a link to your own website.
3. Shoppers who find you through Hadam are directed to your own website to complete their purchase — Hadam does not process payments or orders for Marketplace-only members.
4. You can upgrade to a full Hadam Store at any time — your business information carries over, and you'll gain a subdomain, catalog, cart, checkout, and every other Hadam Store feature.

How it works

1. Enter your store's subdomain and your own admin login for that store.
2. Confirm payment for Marketplace access.
3. Your existing store — same subdomain, same catalog, same customers — is immediately listed in Hadam's shared Marketplace directory.

No second store or subdomain is ever created. If your admin account has two-factor authentication enabled, use your store's own Admin panel instead of this page.

How it works

1. Choose whether you're renewing a Hadam Store subscription or a Marketplace membership.
2. Confirm your identity — your store's admin login, or the email and business name your Marketplace application was submitted with.
3. Payment is verified before anything changes — selecting a plan alone never activates or extends a subscription.
4. Once payment is confirmed, your expiration date is extended by 30 days, and access is restored immediately if it had lapsed.
