Looking to shop, or manage a store you already have?

This page (the Hadam homepage) is not where clients, employees, or store admins log in. Every store has its own address and its own login — go to yourname.hadam.com (or yourname.localhost:3000 in local testing) and log in there. The "Superadmin log in" button above is only for Hadam's own platform staff, who oversee the directory of stores below — it has no access to any store's products, orders, or customers.

How it works

1. Pick a subdomain and a plan — Store, Marketplace, or both.
2. You become the store's Admin — add your own categories and products.
3. Invite Employees (or additional Admins) to help fulfil orders and bookings.
4. Clients register on your subdomain and shop only your catalog.

Every store's data — users, catalog, orders — is fully isolated from every other store.
